
## Push Up Guide


1. **Set up your base:** Proper hand and foot placement protects your wrists and shoulders.
Place your hands slightly wider than shoulder-width apart, stacked directly under your shoulders. Splay your fingers slightly and press through your palms to screw your hands firmly into the floor. Set your feet hip-width or together behind you.


2. **Lock in full-body tension:** Prevents lower back sagging and power leaks.
Squeeze your glutes, brace your core like you are about to take a punch, and press your heels back. Maintain a neutral spine where your head, upper back, and hips form a single straight line.


3. **Lower with controlled elbow position:** Keep elbows at a 45-degree angle, not flared outward.
Inhale as you bend your elbows, lower your entire body in one solid piece, and pull your shoulder blades together. Tuck your elbows so your upper arms form an arrow shape ($\approx 45^\circ$) with your torso—avoid flaring them out to a $90^\circ$ "T" position. Stop when your chest is 1–2 inches off the ground.


4. **Press away and protract:** Finish strong at the top of the movement.
Exhale and drive through your palms to press the floor away, keeping your core rigid. At the top, fully extend your arms and slightly spread your shoulder blades apart (protraction) without rounding your spine.


### Common Mistakes to Fix

* **Flaring elbows out:** Strains the rotator cuff. Keep them angled inward toward your ribs.
* **Sagging hips:** Indicates a relaxed core. Engage your glutes and abs continuously.
* **Craning the neck:** Dropping your chin toward the floor gives a false sense of depth. Keep your gaze angled slightly forward to maintain a neutral neck.
