## Workout Routines

### 1. Full-Body Strength Routine (Mon / Wed / Fri)

*Perform 3 sets of 20 to 30 reps per exercise. Rest 120 seconds between sets.*

1. **Goblet Squat or Sumo Squat:** Hold one 20 lb dumbbell vertically at your chest. Squat down to a chair or bench, driving through your heels to stand. *(Quads, Glutes)*
2. **Dumbbell Floor Press:** Lie on your back on the floor, holding both 20 lb dumbbells. Press straight up from chest height. *(Chest, Triceps, Front Shoulders)*
3. **Bent-Over Dumbbell Rows:** Hinge at the hips with a flat back, pulling both 20 lb dumbbells toward your hips. *(Upper Back, Lats, Biceps)*
4. **Weighted Calf Raises:** Wear your 8 lb ankle weights and hold one or both 20 lb dumbbells at your sides. Raise up onto your toes, pause at the top, and lower slowly. *(Calves, Lower Legs)*
5. **Dumbbell Overhead Press or Lateral Raises:** Seated or standing, press the dumbbells overhead (or substitute lateral raises if overhead pressing feels tight). *(Shoulders)*
6. **Glute Bridges with Ankle Weights:** Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat. Drive hips up to align with knees and shoulders. *(Glutes, Hamstrings, Core)*

---

### 2. Core & Low-Impact Balance Routine (Tue / Thu / Sat)

*Perform 2–3 rounds of the following flow. Focus on control, stability, and steady breathing.*

1. **Forearm or Elevated Plank:** Hold for 20–45 seconds with a straight body line. Modulate difficulty by placing hands on an elevated surface like a bench or sofa if needed. *(Deep Core, Anti-Extension)*
2. **Tip-Toe Holds (Balance & Ankle Stability):** Stand tall near a wall or chair for light support. Rise as high as possible onto your toes and hold for 15–30 seconds. Repeat 3–4 times. *(Calves, Ankle Stability, Balance)*
3. **Bird-Dogs (With or Without Ankle Weights):** On all fours, extend opposite arm and leg simultaneously until parallel with the floor. Pause for 2 seconds at the top. Perform 8–10 reps per side. *(Lower Back, Core, Glutes)*
4. **Dead Bugs:** Lie on your back with knees at 90 degrees and arms straight up. Slowly lower opposite arm and leg toward the floor without arching your lower back. Perform 8–10 reps per side. *(Lower Abdominals)*
5. **Single-Leg Balance Stand:** Stand on one foot with soft knee bend for 30 seconds per side. Add toe taps forward and backward to increase challenge. *(Proprioception, Ankle/Hip Stabilizers)*

---

### 3. Light Upper/Lower Body & Easy Stretch (Sunday)

*Designed as an easy, high-movement flow to finish hitting muscle groups for the 3rd or 4th time without high fatigue.*

#### Light Upper & Lower Body Flow (15–20 Mins)

* **Ankle Weight Straight-Leg Raises:** Lie on back or side; lift leg under control for 12–15 reps per leg. *(Hip Flexors, Quadriceps, Outer Hips)*
* **Ankle Weight Standing Hamstring Curls:** Stand holding a chair, curl heel toward glutes for 12–15 reps per leg. *(Hamstrings)*
* **Light Dumbbell Bicep Curls & Tricep Kickbacks/Extensions:** 2 sets of 12–15 reps using one or both dumbbells. *(Arms)*
* **Light Scapular Squeezes / Doorway Chest Openers:** Stand tall, squeeze shoulder blades together for 3-second holds. *(Postural Muscles)*

#### Easy Full-Body Stretches (15–20 Mins)

* **Cat-Cow Stretch:** On hands and knees, arch and round spine gently with breath (8–10 cycles).
* **Seated Hamstring & Calf Stretch:** Sit at the edge of a chair, extend one leg straight out with heel down and toes pointing up; lean forward gently at the hips until a soft stretch is felt. Hold for 30 seconds per leg.
* **Doorway Chest Stretch:** Place forearms against a door frame and step forward gently to open chest and front shoulders. Hold 30 seconds.
* **Figure-4 Seated Piriformis Stretch:** Cross one ankle over opposite knee while seated; keep spine long and lean forward slightly. Hold 30 seconds per side.

---

### Form Guidelines & Tips

* **Progressive Load Management:** Your 20 lb dumbbells are solid for compound movements (squats, rows, presses). If 20 lbs feels too heavy for lateral raises or overhead presses, perform those movements with bodyweight or light resistance, focusing on strict form.
* **Warm-Up:** Spend 5 minutes before every strength session doing light mobility work (arm circles, torso twists, gentle marching in place).
* **Hydration & Safety:** Keep water handy and perform balance exercises near a wall or sturdy chair so support is always within reach.
